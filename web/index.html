Ranks jobs for a profile and candidates for a job, and shows why. It ranks and explains; the recruiter decides. Data: open jobs of the test period (US, 2012).

Method comparison

Test period, 47,214 users, evaluate.py.

How a profile becomes 10 jobs

Two-stage recommender.

1. CandidatesContent (bge-small + FAISS), item-item collaborative, state popularity top 50
2. Features12 numbers per job in three groups
3. LightGBM LambdaRankScores every candidate (v2, seed 42)
4. Top 10 + WhyESCO skill matching with evidence snippet

Typed profile answered in about 100 ms once warm (1.3 s first call).
